Method, display guide & limitations
  • JPEG Q10 / Q20 / Q40 / Q60 and 5-bit / 4-bit quantization are synthetic conditions. Available methods depend on the image and condition; CODiff is selected initially when available.
  • The original is provided for evaluation. It was not passed as an additional model reference or pasted into outputs. Clean controls process the unchanged original as their declared input.
  • Images and crops retain native coordinates, including the 1254 × 1254 watercolor. All selected stages and the three-view strip share the same display scale.
  • The saved PNG comparison sheets retain their Japanese headings. Their columns are Original / Degraded input (or clean control) / Restoration output.
